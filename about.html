About Veitenheimer Notifications

Veitenheimer Notifications is a small SMS reminder and notification service operated by Adam Veitenheimer as a sole proprietor in Burlington Township, New Jersey. The service sends reminders, schedule updates, and requested information by text message to subscribers who have explicitly opted in.

What we send

Subscribers receive personal reminders and updates they have asked for — for example, a reminder about an upcoming appointment, a schedule change, or a piece of information they requested. We do not send marketing, advertising, or promotional messages, and we never message anyone who has not opted in.

How consent works

People subscribe by texting START to +1 (855) 503-0408 from their own mobile phone. They receive an immediate confirmation and can reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Full details are in our SMS Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
