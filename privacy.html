What we collect

We collect only the mobile phone numbers of subscribers who opt in, together with any information a subscriber chooses to include in their own messages to us. We use this solely to deliver the reminders, updates, and responses the subscriber requested.

No sharing or selling

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for their own purposes.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help. You may also email atveitenheimer@hotmail.com to be removed.

Data retention

We keep a subscriber's number only for as long as they remain opted in. When you opt out, your number is removed from the active list.
