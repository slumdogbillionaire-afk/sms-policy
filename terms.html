1. Program description

Veitenheimer Notifications delivers personal reminders, schedule updates, and requested information by text message. Messages are sent only to subscribers who have opted in. This is not a marketing or promotional program.

2. How you opt in (consent)

You opt in by texting the keyword START to +1 (855) 503-0408 from your mobile phone. Consent to receive these messages is given directly to this program and is not bundled into, or a condition of, any other agreement, purchase, or service.

Upon opt-in you receive this confirmation:

Veitenheimer Notifications: you're subscribed to reminders & updates. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to unsubscribe.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on the reminders and updates you request.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. This program does not charge a fee; your mobile carrier's standard message and data rates apply.
