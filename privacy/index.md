# Privacy Policy

**Program:** Ember Personal Assistant Notifications — +1 (609) 739-8284
_Last updated: August 6, 2026._

## What we collect

We collect only the mobile phone numbers of recipients who opt in, together with
any information a recipient chooses to include in their own messages to us. We
use this solely to deliver the personal notifications and responses the
recipient requested.

## No sharing or selling

**We do not share, sell, or otherwise provide your mobile phone number or
messaging consent information to any third parties or affiliates for marketing
or promotional purposes.** Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent
will not be shared with any third parties under any circumstances. No mobile
information is shared with third parties or affiliates for their own purposes.

## Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
